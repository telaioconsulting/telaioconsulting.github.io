How we work out the result

With simple rules, written here. It's a two-minute pointer, not an assessment: in the check-up we look at your real business.

AI potential, from 0 to 100

Hours a week are worth up to 50 points: fewer than 5 hours 10, 5 to 15 hours 25, 15 to 40 hours 40, more than 40 hours 50. How repetitive the activities are is worth up to 30: almost always the same 30, similar 18, every case different 6. The activities you chose are worth up to 20: one 10, two 16, three 20.

Readiness, from 25 to 100

Where the information lives is worth up to 40 points: in a management system 40, in tidy spreadsheets 25, scattered 10. The AI you already use is worth up to 30: in everyday tasks 30, people on their own 20, not yet 10. Who follows the project is worth up to 30: the owner 30, a manager 20, nobody 5.

The time you could win back

It's the hours of the band you chose times the share that, on a cautious estimate, AI can take off: 30 to 50% if the activities are almost always the same, 20 to 35% if they are similar, 5 to 15% if every case is different. For "more than 40 hours" we count up to 60. A working year is 46 weeks. It's an estimate, not a promise.

The verdict

Below 40 points of potential: "Today AI has little to do for you". If the information is scattered, or nobody has time to follow the project: "Put things in order first". Otherwise: "You can start now".

Questions about the wizard

Do you keep my answers?

No. The result is worked out in your browser and your answers never reach us. If you accept analytics, we count that the wizard was completed, with the answers as codes (sector, people, activities, hours) and the two scores, never who you are. If you book the check-up from the result, a summary goes into the form's message: you can see and change it before sending.

How accurate is the result?

It's a first pointer, made with the simple rules above. It doesn't know your business: that's why we suggest the check-up.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The wizard and the check-up are free.
