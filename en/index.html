FAQ

Questions we often get

What does Telaio do?

Telaio helps small and mid-sized businesses put AI to work where it creates value. We start from a real problem, choose the right tool, put it to work and stay until it delivers. You have one point of contact, from analysis to a working solution.

What is the AI check-up?

A free 30-minute video call with one of our founders. We show you how AI is changing your industry, find two or three places to start, and tell you plainly whether it's worth it.

How soon will I see results?

Your first AI tool is in use within 4 weeks. The clock starts once you've approved the proposal and we have the access we need.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the problem and the solution. Before we start you get a written proposal with time, cost and limits. The check-up is free.

Do you work with companies outside Italy?

Yes. We work remotely, in English or Italian, on Central European Time.

What about data protection and the EU AI Act?

Before we start, we tell you what data the tool uses, where it's stored and who can see it. We choose solutions with you that fit the GDPR and your company's rules, and we flag what the EU AI Act asks of you. We're not lawyers: we'll tell you when to involve one.
