Cookies and settings

Here you'll find which cookies and tools the site uses, what they're for and how to change your choices.

What the site uses today

Today the site doesn't use profiling cookies or analytics tools.

Your theme choice

If you pick the light or dark theme, the site saves that choice in your browser, so it's there next time. It's a technical tool: no consent is needed. You can delete it whenever you like from your browser settings, in the section about site data.

The booking calendar

The calendar for booking the check-up is provided by Google and only loads when you open it. From then on, Google may use its own cookies. You can read how in Google's privacy policy.

What will change

This page will change when the site starts using measurement tools. When that happens, we'll ask for your consent.
