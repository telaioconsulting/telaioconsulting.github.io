Privacy notice

Here we explain what data we handle when you visit the site or book an AI check-up, why we do it and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Pasquale Silvestre, for the Telaio project. For any question, write to info@telaioconsulting.com.

2. What data we handle

  • Browsing. The service that hosts the site, GitHub Pages, logs visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub's documentation explains this.
  • Booking the check-up. First name, last name, email and your answers to the form, collected through the Google Calendar booking page.
  • Email. The information you send us when you write to us.

3. Why we handle it, and on what basis

  • To organise, prepare and hold the call, and to reply to your emails: steps you ask for before any agreement (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the site secure: legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

4. Who handles it on our behalf

We rely on two outside services that handle data on our behalf: Google, for the booking calendar and the video call, and GitHub, which hosts the site. They may process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards the GDPR requires.

5. How long we keep it

We keep data only for as long as the purposes above require.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict its use, to object to its processing or to receive it in a format you can take elsewhere. Just write to info@telaioconsulting.com.

If you think your data is being handled incorrectly, you can complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

7. Automated decisions

We don't make decisions based solely on automated processing.
